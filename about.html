Our Origin Story

From a Mountain Studio to Your Daily Ritual

Haridra Rituals began in a sunlit studio surrounded by lavender fields in Ojai, California. Frustrated by synthetic fillers and harsh chemical preservatives hidden behind "clean" labels, our founders set out to return skincare back to its purest roots.

We started with a single wooden soap mold, cold-pressed extra virgin olive oil, and organic French lavender buds harvested at peak bloom. Today, our philosophy remains unchanged: every single product is formulated with living plant actives that feed your skin while bringing quiet mindfulness to your everyday routine.

Guiding Principles & Standards

Our 6 Core Commitments to You & Earth

We operate with zero compromise across formulation purity, ethical sourcing, and environmental accountability.

01

Wildcrafted & Organic

We source unrefined, cold-pressed plant oils, floral hydrosols, and wildcrafted herbs directly from ethical organic farms.

02

Small-Batch Potency
